ABOUT QCAAS NET

Understanding the quantum future before it arrives.

Quantum Computing-as-a-Service (QCAAS/QAAS) makes quantum computing resources accessible through cloud platforms, allowing researchers, developers, students, and organizations to experiment without owning quantum hardware.

QCAAS Net is an independent learning and research platform focused on explaining the technology behind the quantum ecosystem — from quantum computing and infrastructure to quantum networking and post-quantum security.

Our goal is to make complex quantum technologies easier to understand while separating practical opportunities from marketing noise.
